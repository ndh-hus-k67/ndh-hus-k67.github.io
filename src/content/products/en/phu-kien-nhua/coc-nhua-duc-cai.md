    The male-female plastic standoff (Nylon Hex Standoff) is an industrial plastic fastening component featuring one external (male) threaded end and one internal (female) threaded end. It is designed to securely mount, space, and electrically insulate printed circuit boards (PCBs) and electronic assemblies.

    </br></br>
    Precision-molded from high-grade engineering Nylon PA66, these standoffs offer excellent electrical insulation, lightweight performance, corrosion resistance, and durability in humid or industrial environments. The hexagonal body allows easy tightening with standard wrenches or by hand.

    </br></br>
    Available in standard metric thread sizes from M2, M2.5, M3, M4, M5 to M6 and body lengths (L) ranging from 5mm to 50mm, male-female plastic standoffs can also be stacked together for multi-tier PCB installations, electrical cabinets, and automation equipment.

    </br></br>
    Key technical specifications:

    </br></br>
    - Material: Engineering Nylon PA66 (UL94 V-2 / V-0 flame retardant).
    </br>
    - Standard Colors: Natural White and Black.
    </br>
    - Design: Hexagonal body, male-to-female threaded ends.
    </br>
    - Metric Threads: M2, M2.5, M3, M4, M5, M6.
    </br>
    - Applications: PCB mounting, control boards, electrical cabinets, telecom equipment, and industrial automation.
